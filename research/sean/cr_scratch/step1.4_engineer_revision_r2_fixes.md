**Clause (6c), Design Notes 35 R4 (rule text).**
- Premise: as clause (2): every unsectioned row is a solid of revolution with its axis in the plane, drawn whole (K8-B73, K5). Its front half hides what lies inside its outline, at u where its front surface is nearer than an unsectioned target's.
- Kinds (`artist_kind`): filled area (Patch, fill, no hatch); hatched area (Patch with a hatch); solid stroke (solid Line2D, or a solid unfilled Patch); dashed stroke (a renderer hidden copy, area or Line2D). Spec-dashed artists (K5 sub-artist forms such as sweep, root, slits and the x_band wing, and beyond rows under convention 3) are dashed by their own clause and are not read (9, 9 and 3 drawn).
- Read: a part artist is any tagged Patch or Line2D of a row that is not reference, line, arrow or callout (sub-artists and :outline lines included). A Patch is sampled at its interior (depth > TOL_Z) and along its edge, a Line2D along its polyline, every 0.5 mm.
- "Hidden" (`_front_hides`): inside, or within TOL_Z of, the ring of an occluder of another row. For a cut or beyond parent containment is enough. For an unsectioned parent the occluder must also be in front at u = p[0] (x_front(O) - x_front(T) > TOL_Z, the `_hidden_stroke` sign test).
- Solid kinds: every sample outside the artist's drawn (clip) region is hidden. An artist with no clip is drawn whole and has nothing to read.
- Dashed stroke: every sample inside the copy's drawn region is hidden. A copy with no clip counts as the whole Axes.
- The forward direction of each kind is (6a) and (6b): areas, plus strokes by the line family (TP1.4-5).
- Scope: section Axes (render_section, S3 and S4). S1 side views are carried by name: they have no occlusion clause. Plan views use the plan split, judged by K4 cover (e1, e2). The S3 inset plan has renderer copies from the plan split, and the K8 section convention does not run there.
