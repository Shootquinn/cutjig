## 3 Tick admission rule and premise

`sketch_common.slit_issues` for `<row>:slits`. Scope: every row with `slits` in sub_artists, in a side or section view. Today that is collet and collet_105 (`same_as: collet`).

**Premise.** A slit behind the plane is drawn as:
- one dashed vertical line per declared slit, from its open face to its closed end;
- a horizontal tick at the closed end.

The line states nothing else.

**Branches.**
- (n) count: count_drawn vertical lines and as many ticks, one per spec line, every artist a two-vertex Line2D. Each drawn line is read as the spec line with the nearest z mid.
- (i) sign: sin(theta) x u > 0.
- (ii) containment: both ends inside the convex hull of the row's drawn pieces on the line's side, within 0.05.
- (iii) open face: the open end sits at the side pieces' z extreme and on a horizontal face edge there (face_spans).
- (iv) dashed.
- (t) the closed end at the spec z, with one tick there, tick_length long and centred on the line.

The undeclared case uses the existing sub_artists branch (K5-B50).
