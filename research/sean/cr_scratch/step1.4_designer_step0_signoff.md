## 1 Rule (l): FIX

### 1.1 Narrowing request (orchestrator): refused, one relief granted

A leader whose own anchor lies on a gridline is the worst case of the class: the arrow end sits on the line, so the line reads as ending at the part and the leader disappears into it. S2 base_plate (y 0), S2 screw_b (y 80) and S3 inset screw_b (y 80, 195.4 mm) are that case; the S3 inset leader is a 195 mm grid row drawn darker. No relief for "target on the gridline", and none for plan gridlines (the Engineer's option (c)): plan gridlines read exactly as section gridlines, the M1 S3 r5 finding.

Relief granted (FIX L3, own-line continuation): a label leader is exempt against a line L when (1) the labelled row is a line row (kind line), (2) the leader's anchor is within 0.05 of an end of an open polyline of the row's own ink, (3) the leader leaves that end away from the ink (dot product of ink direction and text direction from the anchor < 0), and (4) that ink segment runs along L under the rule's own test (within 2 deg, within the distance threshold, over more than the run threshold). Premise: L lies along the row's own position, so the leader continues the row's line out to its label at the row's coordinate; the merge carries the reader to the right referent and cannot be misread as another part. This is the drafting form for labelling a datum at its end. Area rows never get it. It relieves S1 height_target (4 poses, line z 2000 on gridline z 2000) and S2 slot_line_c (line y 259, 1.0 data mm under gridline y 260; the step 0 firing at exactly 1.00 mm). Dry run: with L1 to L3 both read 0 firings, every other firing unchanged (MEASURED, `fire.py`, `dry_variants.py`).
